Platform Operations & Subscription Management Center

Comprehensive Administrative User Guide & Operational Procedures for Super Admins and Platform Developers.

1. System Overview & Role Access

The Platform Operations & Subscription Management Center (located at /platform/operations/subscriptions) is the central command center for the Labour Educational Report System across Ghana Basic Schools. It provides administrators with total oversight over subscription entitlements, learner billing calculations, wallet ledgers, and revenue realization.

User Role Access Rights Primary Responsibilities
super_admin Full Control Pricing controls, billing cycle triggers, cycle reverts, exemptions, top-ups.
developer Full Control + Audit Database migrations, schema maintenance, security audit logs, diagnostics.
accountant Read & Financial Ledger Revenue verification, Paystack reconciliation, wallet top-ups, CSV reporting.
operations Read & Category Config School category tagging, learner count monitoring, exemption status.

2. Executive KPI Dashboard & Real-Time Indicators

At the top of the interface, the Executive KPI Metrics Bar provides real-time counts across all onboarded schools:

  • Total Schools: Total count of registered basic schools on the platform.
  • Free Onboarding: Schools running their 16-week trial (First Term Free).
  • Wallet Balance: Cumulative pre-funded GHS wallet balances across all active schools.
  • Frozen Accounts: Schools whose wallet balance is below their term subscription dues, resulting in report card locks.
  • Exempted: Schools granted explicit administrative exemption from term fees.

3. Term Subscription Revenue Analytics Engine

The Term Revenue Analytics dashboard calculates real-time revenue performance for any selected term:

  • Total Billed: Total subscription fees required from non-waived schools.
  • Collected: Realized revenue deducted from school wallets or collected via Paystack.
  • Outstanding: Uncollected shortfall due from schools with insufficient funds.
  • Realization Rate (%): Percentage ratio of collected vs. billed revenue ((Collected / Billed) * 100%).

CSV Revenue Export: Click Revenue Report CSV to download a complete breakdown per school containing learner counts, effective rates, dues, wallet balances, and shortfall metrics.

4. School Subscription Status Matrix & Card Anatomy

The Subscription Matrix displays all registered schools in an interactive 3D Card Grid Format or Table View.

School Card Layout Components:

  1. Header: Displays School Name, ID Badge (e.g. SCH-0042), Category (Private/GES/International), and Running Term Badge (e.g. 📅 Term 1 (2025/2026)).
  2. Status Banner:
    • 🎁 Waived (Free First Term Active)
    • 🟢 Paid & Approved (Term Bill Settled)
    • ⏳ Payment Requested by Admin (Pending Approval)
    • 🔴 Bill Unpaid (Reports & Cards Frozen)
    • 🛡️ Waived (Admin Exempted)
  3. Metrics Box (2x2): Displays Active Learner Count, Rate per Learner, Term Fee, Wallet Balance, and Outstanding Dues.
  4. Action Footer:
    • 💳 Top Up: Performs manual admin credit deposit into school wallet.
    • 🛡️ Exempt / ✅ Exempted: Toggles term exemption for the school.
    • 📜 Bills: Opens billing snapshot history for the school.
    • ⚙️ Config: Opens custom rate override and category settings.

5. Term Billing Trigger & Cycle Revert Controls

5.1 Initiating a Billing Cycle

Under ⚙️ Term Billing Controls (Tab 3), enter the Academic Year (e.g. 2025/2026), select the Term, set the Payment Deadline, and click ⚡ START TERM BILLING. Immutable billing snapshots will be generated for all eligible schools.

5.2 Reverting a Billing Trigger

If a billing cycle was triggered by mistake or requires adjustment:

  1. Go to ⚙️ Term Billing Controls (Tab 3).
  2. Click ⏪ REVERT BILLING TRIGGER (or click ⏪ Revert next to any active cycle in the list).
  3. Unpaid term bill snapshots for that cycle are deleted, allowing you to adjust school exemptions or pricing and re-trigger billing cleanly.

6. School Wallet Management & Manual Top-Ups

  1. On the school's card, click 💳 Top Up.
  2. Enter the Deposit Amount (GH₵), Reference Code, and Deposit Notes.
  3. Click Confirm Top Up. The school's balance updates immediately.
